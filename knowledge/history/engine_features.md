Windows downloads the `-setup.exe` asset (only URLs under this repo's release downloads), verifies it against `SHA256SUMS.txt`, runs it and exits; macOS opens the release page. Not tested end to end yet: needs a published release newer than the installed one.
- 2026-10-17: Gemini 4 Argon weekly allowance (`gemini-argon-wtu-weekly`) is stored per pool account (`argonWeekly`, `argonWeeklyResetAt`, `argonReadAt`) and shown as a wide chip on the pool cards. App backdrop, title bar, sidebar, top bar, scrollbars, Settings selects, switches and the active Settings tab changed from purple/grey to the cards' rose-burgundy.
- 2026-10-17: The browser page after Google sign-in (port 8045 callback) uses the app's starlit pink theme; template in `src-tauri/src/oauth_callback.html`, filled by `callback_page` in `antigravity.rs`. The error page uses the same template.
